- **Changed: filing commits the capture and ship commits the issue folder to devDoc (#1055).** With `commit_devdoc = true` and the devDoc in git, `/devagent:file` commits `Captures/<slug>/` (with its `filed.toml`) and step 18 (ship) commits the issue's devDoc folder, on the zero-diff skip too, each under the devDoc lock and pathspec-scoped to that folder alone, so nothing else is swept in. Both commits are best effort: a busy lock (10 s by default), an operation in progress, a detached HEAD or a failed commit prints one `devdoc commit of <dir> skipped` warning with the hand commit, and the step's exit is unchanged. Cleanup still commits the issue folder's later changes.
