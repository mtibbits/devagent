- **Added: required CI checks as acceptance evidence (#1097).** A project lists the CI checks it requires with `required_checks` (and, optionally, `required_checks_paths`) under `[project.<name>]`. Step 20 (mergemr) merges only when each is `success` at the verified head, turns forge auto-merge off while one is not, stops `[!]` `CI NOT CHECKED` (rule 20) on a code backend that cannot report checks, and stops on a self-certifying diff until the operator acknowledges it in `.devagent-selfcert-ack` (rule 21). Preship's verifier records a criterion such a check meets as `deferred to CI at merge: <check>` after `scripts/preship-deferred.sh` checks its static half at the pinned head, and capture writes that criterion into a new issue's draft. `mr-checks` `pass` lines now end in the check's conclusion.
