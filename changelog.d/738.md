- **Added: plans record an estimate, and impact compares it with the actuals (#738).** The scope step now writes `- Estimate (scope): {est: <agent time>, tok: <tokens>}` under the plan's `### Size estimate` heading (`est` agent wall time in `h`, `d` or `w`; `tok` input-token equivalents in `k` or `M`; one value or a `low-high` range), and the tighten step always writes `- Estimate (confirmed): {…}` directly below it, changed or not. The new read-only `scripts/plan-estimate.sh [--json | --impact-line] <imPlan.md>` (core `scripts/lib/plan-estimate.py`) reads the last such line back: exit 0 present, 1 absent (no structured estimate, as every older plan reads), 3 unreadable (an estimate-shaped line that does not parse), 2 usage or unreadable file, 4 internal error. The impact step runs it and records one `- Estimate vs actual (interim): …` line in `impact.md` after the Workflow lines, with the estimate beside the agent wall time and input-token equivalents it just recorded, and computes no ratio. Spec §13.7 holds the contract; FR-114 is no longer planned except for copying the estimate onto the issue's WBS leaf.
